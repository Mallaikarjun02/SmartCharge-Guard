# User Research Plan

## Objective
Validate whether charging unpredictability and poor explanation are meaningful customer problems before committing engineering resources.

## Sample
8–15 EV owners across:
- daily commuters
- frequent fast-charging users
- long-distance travellers
- battery-conscious owners

## Interview questions
1. Tell me about your last charging session.
2. What makes a charging session successful?
3. Have you experienced charging slower than expected?
4. What did you think caused it?
5. How did the vehicle communicate the change?
6. How important is charging ETA accuracy?
7. What would make you trust an automatic charging adjustment?
8. Would you trade some peak speed for better predictability or battery longevity?
9. Which charging information is useful?
10. What would make you contact support?

## Research rules
- Do not lead the respondent.
- Capture exact quotes.
- Separate observed behaviour from opinions.
- Record frequency and severity.
- Do not invent findings.
