# Analytics & KPI Framework

## North Star
**Thermal-related charging sessions completed successfully without customer-reported dissatisfaction.**

## Customer KPIs
- Charging ETA accuracy
- Charging-related support contacts / 1,000 sessions
- Notification comprehension
- Charging satisfaction

## Technical KPIs
- Thermal protection events / 1,000 sessions
- Charging derating duration
- Charging completion rate
- Charging session abandonment

## Business KPIs
- Warranty/service incidents related to charging
- Support cost
- Customer satisfaction

## Release KPIs
- OTA installation success
- Rollback rate
- Crash/error rate
- Feature adoption

## Event taxonomy
`charging_session_started`
`charging_state_changed`
`adaptive_charging_started`
`adaptive_charging_ended`
`eta_updated`
`customer_notification_shown`
`customer_notification_opened`
`charging_completed`
`charging_abandoned`
`ota_update_started`
`ota_update_completed`
`ota_rollback`
