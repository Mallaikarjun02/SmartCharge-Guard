# Product Strategy

## Vision
**Make EV charging feel predictable even when physical conditions are not.**

## Mission
Use validated vehicle data and software intelligence to optimize charging behaviour within approved operating limits while clearly communicating meaningful changes to the customer.

## Target users
Primary: consumer EV owners using fast charging.
Secondary: customer support, service operations and EV product teams.

## Product principles
1. Safety first.
2. Predictability over peak-performance marketing.
3. Explain, don't alarm.
4. Graceful degradation.
5. Progressive automation.
6. Measure outcomes.

## Goals
- Improve charging ETA accuracy.
- Reduce avoidable charging-related support contacts.
- Improve customer understanding of charging adjustments.
- Maintain successful charging completion.
- Enable safe staged OTA deployment.

## Non-goals
- Replacing BMS safety logic.
- Direct mobile override of safety controls.
- Guaranteeing maximum charging speed.
- Claiming universal temperature thresholds.

## Positioning
**For EV owners experiencing variable charging performance, SmartCharge Guard is an adaptive charging experience that explains charging changes and updates ETA while remaining within validated vehicle limits.**

## Roadmap
### Phase 1 — MVP
- Charging-state classification
- Dynamic ETA
- Simple thermal-aware notification
- Charging event telemetry
- OTA monitoring

### Phase 2
- Charging history
- Battery-health education
- Improved prediction
- Service diagnostics

### Phase 3
- Predictive pre-conditioning
- Personalized charging recommendations
- Fleet/product analytics
