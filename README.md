# SmartCharge Guard ⚡
### Adaptive EV Charging & Thermal-Aware Customer Experience

**Product Management Portfolio Case Study | EV / Battery / Connected Vehicles**

SmartCharge Guard is a proposed OTA-enabled EV charging experience that connects validated battery/thermal telemetry with adaptive charging, dynamic charging ETA, customer communication, analytics, and controlled software rollout.

> **Important:** This is a product-management case study, not a production safety controller. Safety-critical limits remain within validated BMS/vehicle-control systems. Temperature thresholds and performance targets shown in this repository are illustrative until validated by vehicle engineering.

## Problem
EV charging performance can vary because of battery temperature, ambient conditions, charger capability, SoC and other physical constraints. When the customer does not understand why charging slows down or why the ETA changes, a normal protective behaviour can be perceived as a vehicle defect.

## Product hypothesis
**Make EV charging feel predictable even when physical conditions are not.**

SmartCharge Guard aims to:
- provide transparent adaptive charging within validated limits
- dynamically update charging ETA
- explain meaningful charging changes in simple language
- capture charging-event telemetry for product/service teams
- support staged OTA deployment and rollback
