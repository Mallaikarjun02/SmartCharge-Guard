# Resume / Interview Positioning

## Resume bullets
- Conducted market and competitor research across EV charging, BMS, thermal management, telematics and OTA ecosystems to identify a customer-experience opportunity.
- Developed SmartCharge Guard, an adaptive EV charging product concept connecting validated vehicle telemetry with dynamic charging ETA, transparent customer communication and staged OTA rollout.
- Created product strategy, PRD, user stories, acceptance criteria, KPI framework, technical architecture and UX wireframe specifications.
- Defined North Star and supporting metrics linking charging experience to customer satisfaction, support volume, reliability and service outcomes.

## Interview one-liner
“I did not approach this as a battery-engineering project. I approached it as a product problem: how can an EV manufacturer turn unavoidable charging constraints into a predictable and trustworthy customer experience?”

## Questions to prepare
1. Why is this a product problem?
2. Who is the primary user?
3. Why would an OEM build it?
4. What is the MVP?
5. How do you measure success?
6. What happens if the internet is unavailable?
7. Why not let the BMS handle everything?
8. How would you validate the problem?
9. What would make you kill the idea?
10. How would you roll it out safely?
